Structured reviews

Frame review questions, organize themes, compare findings, identify evidence gaps, and plan narrative or scoping reviews. Systematic reviews still require reproducible searches and a predefined protocol.

Critical review

Assess rationale, novelty, study design, methods, statistical reporting, interpretation, limitations, and the fit between conclusions and results.

03 / REFERENCES

Source verification

Check bibliographic details and DOI records when tools are available, and clearly flag citations or claims that remain unverified.

04 / WRITING

Academic editing

Improve argument structure, precision, coherence, abstracts, titles, responses to reviewers, and journal-specific presentation without changing results or overstating claims.

05 / METHODS

Research planning

Refine aims, hypotheses, conceptual frameworks, methods descriptions, analysis plans, and reporting checklists appropriate to the study.

06 / REVISION

Publication readiness

Prioritize revisions, address reviewer feedback, strengthen limitations, and check consistency across the manuscript and supplementary materials.

Shared method

Define. Evaluate. Verify. Interpret.

A common discipline connects academic writing with natural-product evidence assessment, without conflating their specific outputs or levels of validation.

1. Define

Clarify the question, scope, audience, and intended output.

2. Evaluate

Assess source quality, methods, findings, and evidence gaps.

3. Verify

Check references, data provenance, consistency, and claim strength.

4. Interpret

Present qualified conclusions, uncertainties, and next steps for expert review.

Responsible use

Clear boundaries. Human judgment remains central.

These resources support research and education; they do not replace expert judgment, formal peer review, statistical consultation, ethics review, or journal requirements. Generated citations, DOI metadata, quotations, factual claims, and analytical outputs must be checked against original sources. PhytoPrecision is a developing framework and is not represented here as a clinically validated or regulator-approved system. Neither platform should be used as a substitute for individualized medical assessment or treatment decisions. Users remain responsible for research integrity, authorship, disclosure of AI assistance where required, and all submitted or applied conclusions.
