Research support across the full workflow

More than manuscript review.

Use the assistant to plan, evaluate, write, and refine academic work. Outputs should be checked against the original sources and the requirements of the target journal or institution.

01 / LITERATURE

Structured literature reviews

Develop review questions, organize themes, compare findings, identify evidence gaps, and build a transparent outline for narrative or scoping reviews. Systematic reviews require a predefined protocol and reproducible searches.

02 / MANUSCRIPTS

Critical manuscript review

Evaluate novelty, rationale, study design, methods, statistical reporting, interpretation, limitations, tables, figures, and alignment between evidence and conclusions.

03 / WRITING

Academic writing and editing

Improve clarity, structure, argumentation, transitions, abstracts, titles, responses to reviewers, and journal-specific presentation without inventing results or overstating claims.

04 / REFERENCES

Reference and DOI checks

Check whether cited references and DOI records can be verified, identify incomplete or mismatched bibliographic details, and flag sources that still need direct confirmation.

05 / RESEARCH DESIGN

Research questions and methods

Refine research aims, conceptual frameworks, hypotheses, methodology descriptions, analysis plans, and reporting checklists appropriate to the study type.

06 / REVISION

Publication-readiness support

Prepare section-by-section revision priorities, assess reviewer comments, strengthen discussion and limitations, and check consistency across the manuscript and supplementary materials.

A structured scientific approach

Natural products deserve careful context.

Research quality depends on the question, study design, source reliability, methods, and strength of inference. The assistant separates established findings from interpretation, uncertainty, and claims requiring verification.

01 / EVIDENCE

Evidence appraisal

Assess study design, source quality, consistency, limitations, and whether the conclusions are supported by the reported results.

02 / SYNTHESIS

Critical synthesis

Compare studies carefully, distinguish association from causation, and avoid treating preliminary or conflicting evidence as settled fact.

03 / VERIFICATION

Traceable sources

Prioritize identifiable papers and DOI records, and clearly flag citations or factual details that could not be independently confirmed.

A transparent workflow

From question to qualified insight.

A practical framework for moving from an academic question to a more rigorous and clearly communicated research output.

1. Define

Clarify the research question, objectives, audience, and target output.

2. Review

Examine sources, methods, findings, and evidence gaps.

3. Verify

Check citations, DOI details, internal consistency, and claim strength.

4. Refine

Present a structured synthesis with limitations and clear revision priorities.

Responsible use

Scientific education, with clear boundaries.

Dr. Mahmoud Scholar GPT provides research and educational support. It can make mistakes, and generated citations, DOI metadata, quotations, and factual claims must be checked against the original publication or authoritative record before use. It must not fabricate sources, data, analyses, or study findings. It does not replace expert judgment, formal peer review, a statistician, an ethics committee, or journal instructions. Users remain responsible for research integrity, authorship, disclosure of AI assistance where required, and final submitted content.
